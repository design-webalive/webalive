About Reflexion Finish

Young & Energetic Team.
Two Decades of Expertise.

We deliver an unforgettable service, with a combination of great experience, a professional team, best materials and an exceptional eye for detail.

We at Reflexion Finish are a young and energetic team with over 20 years of industry experience. Our goal is to preserve, beautify, and protect your home or business with quality painting products and service.

Our team of highly skilled painters will get the job right, the first time, so you can enjoy your home or business for many years to come. A fresh coat of paint can make an amazing difference to any home or office — from one feature wall in a striking colour to a complete interior transformation.

Reflexion Finish is a Dulux accredited business, therefore we only use quality paints for guaranteed results. Whatever your painting requirements — home, office, clubs, schools, interior, exterior — we would be more than happy to assist you.

How It Works

The Reflexion Process.

A proven four-step methodology that guarantees exceptional results on every single project.

01
Surface Assessment

Every project starts with a thorough analysis of the substrate, existing coatings, and environmental conditions.

02
Preparation & Priming

Correct surface prep is everything. We clean, repair, and prime to manufacturer specification before any topcoat is applied.

03
Precision Application

Professional-grade techniques ensure even, full-coverage coats with no holidays, runs, or sags.

04
Quality Sign-Off

Final inspection across gloss level, film thickness, and adhesion. Every job signed off to our exacting standards.
